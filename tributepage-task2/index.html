Biography

Vincent Willem van Gogh was a Dutch Post-Impressionist painter who became one of the most influential figures in Western art history. Though he struggled with poverty and mental illness during his lifetime, his artwork later became world-famous for its emotional depth, bold colors, and expressive brushwork.

Van Gogh created more than 2,000 artworks, including landscapes, portraits, and still-life paintings. Some of his most famous works include Starry Night, Sunflowers, and The Bedroom. His unique artistic style continues to inspire artists and art lovers all over the world.
